The screenshot gets most of the editor. A compact dock pairs familiar icons with labels, while details and text extraction come forward only when needed.

WHAT CHANGES

  • Two compact capture tiles remove the row chrome.
  • A small dock pairs icons with labels for frequent tools.
  • The OCR rail is easy to close when space matters.

TRADEOFF

The dock must avoid covering small captures and needs strong keyboard and tooltip support.
